# Code of conduct

This project is small and run by one maintainer. This page says how everyone
taking part is expected to behave — in issues, pull requests, discussions and
anywhere else the project is represented — and what happens when someone does
not.

## Expected

- Be considerate and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Disagree about the work, not about the person. Quote the rule, the vector or
  the line you mean.
- Accept a decline with its reason; ask again with new evidence, not louder.
- Say when you used a software assistant, as `CONTRIBUTING.md` describes.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Unwanted sexual attention, and threats of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Deliberate disruption: flooding, spam, or reopening a settled thread without
  anything new.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock a thread, close an issue or a
pull request, and block anyone who breaks these rules, for as long as it takes.
Serious or repeated cases are blocked permanently.

## Reporting

Report a problem through the contact page at
<https://andreibesleaga.com/contact/>. Say where it happened and link to it.
Reports are read by the maintainer only and are not published.
