## Prose-only rules

The reasons are a closed list:

- **definition** — the rule defines a term, a name or a vocabulary that other rules then check.
- **scope** — the rule states what the specification covers or leaves out; there is no behaviour to test.
- **pointer** — the rule points to another rule or document that carries the obligation.
- **person** — the rule binds a person, an operator or a publisher, not software output.
- **forge** — the rule is enforced by the forge or the hosting platform, outside the engine.
- **deferred** — the rule reserves or permits a behaviour for a later version; nothing is emitted at 1.0.
- **adapter** — the rule binds a model, channel or platform adapter that the reference distribution does not ship.
